Dr. Ayesha Rahman is a double board-certified clinical cardiologist with advanced specialization in non-invasive cardiovascular imaging, women’s heart disease prevention, and post-infarction rehabilitation. She completed her Internal Medicine residency at Johns Hopkins University followed by a dedicated Cardiovascular Medicine Fellowship at Harvard Medical School / Brigham & Women’s Hospital.

Her care ethos emphasizes collaborative, zero-stress communication, ensuring patients understand hemodynamic risk scores, stress testing metrics, and sustainable lifestyle interventions. She serves as Clinical Assistant Professor and Active Attending at Manhattan Cardiovascular Center.
